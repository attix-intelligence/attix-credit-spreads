## Twin fidelity

Registry lists `backtest_config: configs/champion.json`, but that is the multi-strategy *optimizer* artifact (credit_spread + iron_condor + momentum_swing + debit_spread + zero_dte); the **deployed** paper scanner (`configs/paper_champion.yaml`) runs only CS+IC, and the engine supports only CS+IC. This run is an engine twin of the deployed YAML: CS+IC, DTE 15–25 (target 15), 2 % OTM, 12-wide, PT 55 %, SL 1.25×, **17 % flat risk non-compounding**, max 30 contracts / 10 positions / 4 per expiration, no VIX entry gate (none in the YAML), combo regime (champion G21 parity config, ma_slow 80), IC neutral-regime-only, `drawdown_cb_pct` 40.

**Documented gaps:** live compass VIX-percentile proxy vs engine ComboRegimeDetector (EXP-3570 divergence); `technical.use_trend_filter` (MA 50/80) not represented; engine two-wing IC sizing vs the live one-wing quirk; scan-slot duplicate-entry defect not modeled. **Dominant engine semantic (read before the table):** the engine's `drawdown_cb_pct: 40` breaker in non-compounding mode measures drawdown against *starting capital* — once equity fell through −40 % in 2020 it never recovered, so **entries are blocked permanently from 2020 onward in both runs**. Every number below is effectively "2020, then dead." The live paper account (fresh $100k, Apr-2026 start) never approached −40 %, so live never exercised this breaker; the twin result is an honest statement about the config on 2020 marks, not a replay of the 2026 paper quarter.

**Why marketable is *worse* here (opposite of EXP-800-BT):** there is no Kelly overlay downsizing into the 2020 crash — every fill risks a flat 17 % of $100k. On the naive path the account collapsed through −40 % within 28 trades and the breaker froze it. On the marketable path, many crash-period entries were rejected (fills only at genuinely marketable prices), which kept equity above the cutoff *longer* — so it placed **more** full-size trades into the worst tape of the window before dying deeper (−80.7 %). Honest fills don't rescue a ruinously-sized strategy; they can extend its exposure to ruin.

## Verdict

The champion core at its deployed 17 % flat sizing is **ruinous on real 2020 marks under both fill models** (−61 % naive, −74.5 % marketable, both then breaker-dead for six years). This is consistent with, and even starker than, EXP-800-BT (same signal core with Kelly sizing: −45 to −95 % across variants) — a fourth independent line of evidence that the champion signal family's returns in 2026 paper were regime beta, not edge. Honest fills change nothing directionally for EXP-400 except to show the failure is not a fill-model artifact. Caveat for interpretation: the fixed-reference breaker permanence means these runs measure "champion config meets 2020 at 17 %/trade" rather than a six-year continuously-trading strategy; but a launch case would need that first encounter survived, and it is not.

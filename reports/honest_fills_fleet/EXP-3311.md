### Differentiator port and its calendar

`entry_gate.nfp_filter` skips entries when **tomorrow (next calendar day)** is an NFP release (`shared/entry_gate.should_skip_entry_for_nfp` — calendar-day, so the gate fires on Thursdays). Live reads BLS dates from `configs/event_blacklist.json`, which only covers 2026. The twin uses: the **published 2026 calendar** (`compass/orchestrator/calendars/nfp_2026.csv`) plus a **deterministic BLS-schedule reconstruction for 2020–2025** (first Friday of month; a July-4-holiday Friday shifts to the Thursday before; a Jan-1 Friday shifts to the second Friday). The rule reproduces the published 2026 calendar **12/12** after those two adjustments. 84 NFP dates cover the window. This is a calendar reconstruction, not market data — Rule Zero intact.

### Material sizing divergence — twin UNDERSTATES the live account

The yaml says `max_risk_per_trade: 33.6%`; the engine hard-caps at **25%**. The live paper account really did size to the yaml (38-lot entries ≈ $33–40k max loss, 30–35% of NAV, per the broker-verified go-live review). So this twin risks ~25% per trade where live risks ~34% — every loss figure below is **conservative relative to deployed behavior**.

**Other fidelity gaps (documented, not shimmed):** live compass VIX-percentile regime proxy vs engine ComboRegimeDetector (EXP-3570 fleet-wide gap); live IC class's separate params not modeled; `min_credit_pct=0` (live entry path has no credit floor); `momentum_filter_pct` never applied by the deployed scanner; `stop_loss_pct_of_width` dead key. **Drawdown CB:** yaml 40%, engine CB latches permanently once breached while the deployed CB is in-memory/fail-open and never fired live through a −40.4% June-2026 drawdown on the broker record — disabled here (EXP-800-BT precedent); latching-CB sensitivity below.

## Verdict

The NFP gate does exactly what it says — it blocked 10–11 pre-NFP entry days, the twin analog of the gate that dodged the 2026-06-05 fleet-killer — and it is **irrelevant at this sizing**: risking ~25% of a fixed $100k per trade (live: ~34%), the account is ruined by November 2020 under both fill models, with average losers of −$9k to −$11k that no monthly event calendar can outrun. Honest fills halve the trade count (50% of naive entries never fill, matching EXP-800-BT) and soften the losses, but per-trade quality degrades (win rate −7 pp) and the destination is identical. The registry's Sharpe-4.984/DD-5.89% expectation is provenance-invalid — it describes an event mask on the v8a portfolio, not this strategy — and should be struck. Implication for the fleet discussion: EXP-3311's live +14.7% quarter is regime luck riding uncontrolled sizing (its own −40.4% June DD already said so); the *gate mechanism* deserves to survive into a properly-sized launch candidate, the *strategy as configured* does not. No backtest support under any fill model.

**Why marketable trades MORE, not less (unlike EXP-800-BT):** path dependence through the drawdown breaker. On the naive path, equity crossed −10 % vs starting capital during 2022 and the engine's fixed-reference breaker then blocked **all entries from 2023 onward** (per-year: 2023–2026 all 0.00 %). On the marketable path, ~8.7k per-slot fill-or-cancel rejections meant fewer/different fills, shallower losses, equity mostly above the $90k cutoff — so it kept trading all six years (per-year: 2020 −0.21, 2021 +1.34, 2022 −5.26, 2023 +1.65, 2024 −0.43, 2025 +0.57, 2026-Q1 −3.56). The headline "unfillable" figure is therefore only meaningful on the slot-attempt basis for this experiment; a trade-count basis is sign-inverted by the breaker path effect.

## Verdict

Under both fill models, the EXP-1220 engine twin has **no positive edge on 2020–2026 real marks**: naive −13.9 % (Sharpe −0.24), marketable −5.9 % (Sharpe −0.05). Honest fills shrink the loss (negative-EV entries that never fill are entries you're spared) and per-trade expectancy improves from −$114 to −$10/trade — i.e., **approximately break-even before any edge**, with an 84 % win rate fully consumed by 2.4–3.4 k average losers. The live paper account's +6.2 % quarter (Apr–Jul 2026, −11.8 % MaxDD) sits well above anything this twin produces over any comparable stretch, so either the live quarter was a favorable regime draw, or live behavior (compass proxy, trend filter, scan-slot timing) differs from this twin in ways that matter — the same replication gap flagged fleet-wide by EXP-3570. **Implication for the launch-candidate discussion:** EXP-1220's demonstrated live *risk process* remains real, but this twin provides no 6-year evidence of positive expectancy at deployed parameters; a launch case cannot cite this backtest as edge evidence, and the go/no-go edge gate (positive expectancy net of costs on the honest twin) is currently **not met**.

## Verdict

Honest fills do not rescue EXP-3303B — nothing could, because there is nothing there to rescue: the strategy is the champion signal at 15.3% fixed-dollar risk with a **provably dead** protective gate. Both fill models ruin the account (equity < 0), naive in Nov-2020 and marketable in Dec-2021; honest fills merely slow the bleed (avg loss −$4.4k vs −$7.4k) by rejecting 99.3% of per-slot entry attempts. Two actionable findings: (1) the **regime-gate vocabulary mismatch is a code bug worth fixing or retiring explicitly** — the paper account is paying the cost of a gate that cannot fire; (2) the registry's Sharpe-6.33 "backtest expectation" is provenance-invalid for this strategy and should be struck. On this evidence EXP-3303B's paper deployment has no backtest support under any fill model, consistent with its NO verdict in the go-live ranking.

## Twin status

EXP-3309 had **no pre-existing backtest twin** (`registry: backtest_config: null`). Its registry expectation ("Net Sharpe 5.879, 343 bps/yr cost savings") comes from `compass/reports/exp3309_liquidity_weighted_entry.json` — explicitly a **scenario analysis using literature coefficients** (spread×/slippage×/fill-rate multipliers from Chordia et al. / Doshi-Patel 2025) on the v8a cost model, not a trade-level backtest. The registry itself carries the caveat: "paper deployment is the empirical validation test." Those numbers are not comparable to anything here.

This run is a new **engine twin transcribed from `configs/paper_exp3309.yaml`**: champion core (SPY, DTE 15/15–25, 2% OTM, $12-wide, PT 55%, SL 1.25×, combo regime verbatim, ICs in neutral), 8.5% flat risk non-compounding off $100k, max 25 contracts / 10 positions / 4 per expiration.

### The differentiator IS representable — and was ported

The pre-close window (`execution.window_only`, 15:30–16:00 ET) maps directly onto the engine's 5-min scan-slot grid: entries allowed only at slots 15:30–15:55. The intraday cache (`option_intraday`) covers 1,540 distinct dates 2020-01-02 → 2026-02-24 (near-complete for the window), so both fills and window timing use real 5-min bars; the final ~27 sessions fall back to the daily-bar day-limit path (counted: `fill_model_naive_fallbacks=0` — no naive-booked entries). Gate counters: **44,890 (naive) / 92,665 (marketable) out-of-window slot attempts blocked; 2,576 / 4,860 in-window attempts allowed.** Exits are not window-gated, matching live (only `scan_opportunities` is gated; PositionMonitor runs on its own cadence).

**Fidelity gaps (documented, not shimmed):** live compass VIX-percentile regime proxy vs engine ComboRegimeDetector (EXP-3570 fleet-wide gap); live IC class's separate params not modeled by the engine fallback IC; `min_credit_pct=0` (live entry path has no credit floor); `momentum_filter_pct` never applied by the deployed scanner (disabled); `stop_loss_pct_of_width` is a dead key. **Drawdown CB:** yaml 40%, but the engine CB latches permanently once breached while the deployed CB is in-memory/fail-open and never fired live through −23.2% paper drawdowns — disabled here (EXP-800-BT precedent); latching-CB sensitivity run reported below.

## Verdict — the one partially interesting result in this batch

Both fill models still **ruin the account** — at 8.5% fixed-dollar risk the champion signal grinds to zero through 2022 regardless of execution. But EXP-3309 is the only twin in this fleet batch where the *differentiator visibly helps in the direction its thesis claims*: pre-close entries plus honest fills nearly halve the average loss (−$2,023 vs −$2,805), stretch survival by ~2.4 years, and produce the batch's only positive full year (2023: +18.3%). The 89% slot-rejection rate also confirms the liquidity thesis was directionally right about *when* fills are achievable. That is execution alpha on top of a signal with **negative edge** — a better engine bolted to the same broken car. The registry's Sharpe-5.879 expectation was never a backtest of this strategy and should be struck; the paper deployment (+3.6%, −23.2% DD, "HOLD passive") gains no backtest support from this re-run, but the pre-close window mechanism itself is worth carrying forward into any future launch candidate.

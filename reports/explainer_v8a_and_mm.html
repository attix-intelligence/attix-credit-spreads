① What is v8a?

v8a = our "sell insurance on the market" machine

v8a is our flagship trading strategy. The simplest way to understand it: we act like an insurance company for the stock market.

The analogy. An insurance company sells you car insurance. Most months nothing happens, so they keep your premium. Once in a while there's a crash and they pay out — but they price the premiums so that, on average, they collect more than they pay. v8a does exactly this, but for the stock market.

Investors are nervous — they constantly overpay for protection against a market crash (this is called "options"). v8a sells that protection and collects the premium. History shows people overpay for this fear by a wide margin. That persistent overpayment is our edge — quants call it the "Volatility Risk Premium" (VRP).

② How market making would work here

Market making = stop paying the toll, start collecting it

The analogy. Every time you exchange money at an airport, the booth quotes two prices: it buys dollars cheap and sells them dear, pocketing the gap. That gap is the "spread." A market maker is that currency booth — it doesn't bet on which way prices go; it just earns the spread by being the one everyone trades against.

The key insight for us

Right now, when v8a opens or closes a trade, we are the tourist at the booth — we cross the spread and pay the toll. Options spreads are wide (a $2.00 option might be quoted $1.90 bid / $2.10 ask — that's a 10% gap). We pay a slice of that every single trade.

The upgrade: instead of grabbing the price that's offered, we post our own price just inside the gap and wait a moment to be filled. Now we're the booth, not the tourist. We capture part of the spread we used to pay.

Options & crypto spreads are wide (1–10%), there's far less competition in the corners we'd pick, and speed stops being the thing that decides who wins — smarts and risk discipline do. That's our home turf.
③ What's the earnings potential?

How much could this actually make?

Two separate income sources, stacked:

A) The v8a execution upgrade (the easy win)

This doesn't invent new profit — it stops a leak in the profit we already make. On every trade we currently donate part of a wide spread. Recapturing even a portion is a multi-percent boost to v8a's annual return, for essentially zero added risk. Free money we're currently leaving on the table. And we can test it this week on data we already own.

As an annual return on the $1MM, that's roughly 8–30%+ in good execution — real money, but not the smooth ride v8a is. Expect bumpy days, real drawdowns, and a fat "tail" risk (a sudden gap can hurt a market-maker fast). This is a conditional number: it depends heavily on how well we execute, not just on showing up.

Is $1MM enough? — Yes.

Capital is not the thing holding us back. $1MM is plenty to start. The real limits are: (1) how wide the spreads are where we play, (2) how often we get "picked off" by smarter/faster traders (adverse selection), and (3) how many small books our models and risk system can run well at once. We grow by running more small books, not by betting bigger on one.

2. The Core Idea — Regime-Based Kelly

Kelly sizing says: bet a fraction of capital proportional to your edge. Full Kelly maximizes long-run growth but is famously violent — it tolerates 50–80% drawdowns on the way to big returns. EXP-800 deliberately bets fractional Kelly, and the fraction shrinks as the market gets riskier:

7%

4%

The Kelly math underneath: base win rate 79.1%, payoff ratio b = 0.403, break-even win rate 71.3%. The edge is real but thin in payoff terms — which is exactly why oversizing is dangerous and the circuit breaker matters.

3. The 3-Tier Circuit Breaker (the “4/7/9” / live “8/10/12”)

Drawdown is measured live from the running equity peak before every new entry. As DD deepens, sizing is throttled, then halted. Recovery is automatic — there is no manual reset; as equity climbs back toward the peak, the tiers lift on the very next trade.

TierDD Trigger (live config)Action
Normal> −8%Full regime Kelly (4–9%)
Tier 1≤ −8%Halve position sizing
Tier 2≤ −10%Floor sizing to 2%
Tier 3≤ −12%Halt all new entries for 30 trades

Note: the backtest report (safe_kelly_report.md) swept thresholds and recommended the tighter 4/7/9 config (hence the name); the deployed configs/paper_exp800.yaml currently runs the slightly looser 8/10/12 tiers. Both target a sub-12% max drawdown.

5. Strengths

  • Drawdown is the headline feature. The whole point is bounded DD (target <−12%) with automatic, lockout-free recovery.
  • Built on a validated signal. EXP-400 Champion (robustness 0.870) is the engine — EXP-800 only changes how much, not what.
  • Regime-aware. Risk auto-cuts from 9% → 4% as the regime turns bearish, plus a hard VIX>40 kill switch.
  • No ML, no overfitting surface. Pure rules (MA80 + RSI + VIX). Easy to audit, hard to break.
  • Live results track the thesis: +43% since late March on bounded risk, with the equity curve showing the breaker doing its job during the early-June dip then recovering.

6. Risks & Honest Caveats

Win rate optics — read carefully. The card shows a 29% live win rate, which looks alarming but is a small-sample artifact: only 7 trades have closed while 10 are still open, and credit spreads that hit the 55% profit target early may not yet be counted. Backtest win rate is 71–95% by year. Do not judge this strategy on 7 closed trades.
  • Sequential-DD approximation. The backtest models trades sequentially; with multiple simultaneously open SPY spreads, true live portfolio DD can differ from the simulated number. Live equity tracking across all legs is the real safeguard.
  • Single underlier concentration. 100% SPY. No diversification across the 8 alpha streams that v8a uses. A SPY-specific gap risk is undiversified here.
  • Sharpe is modest (0.53 backtest). This is a survival-tuned variant, not a Sharpe-optimized one. It trades raw growth for drawdown control.
  • Defined-risk but assignment/gap tail. Spreads cap max loss per position, but stop-loss at 1.25× credit can still stack losses in a fast selloff before the breaker engages.
  • Look-ahead in backtest stats. The report notes b and base win rate were computed on the full dataset; a strict no-look-ahead version would have slightly lower early-year precision.
  • Operational fragility (now fixed): On 2026-03-28 a pre-market entry left an order stuck in pending_open forever (market-closed guard didn’t clean up the DB). Root-caused and patched: failed-open transitions on every early-return path + 60-min stale-pending auto-recovery. Worth knowing as a live-execution gotcha.

7. Benefits — Who/What It’s For

  • Capital-preservation sleeve. When the mandate is “compound steadily, never have a heart-attack drawdown,” this is the design.
  • Crash-survivable. 2020 and 2022 stress years both stayed inside ~−13% DD while remaining net positive.
  • Self-governing. The breaker means it won’t need babysitting in a selloff — it throttles and halts itself, then re-engages on recovery.
  • Composable. As a SPY workhorse it slots cleanly alongside the broader v8a portfolio (XLF/XLI/GLD/SLV streams) for diversification.

8. Bottom Line

EXP-800 is a defense-first deployment of a proven SPY credit-spread edge. It deliberately sacrifices raw Kelly’s eye-watering returns to guarantee a survivable drawdown, using regime-scaled sizing (9/7/4%) and a 3-tier circuit breaker. Live: +43% since inception on bounded risk. The 29% live win rate is a 7-trade small-sample artifact, not a red flag. The real risks are SPY concentration, sequential-DD approximation vs. true multi-leg portfolio DD, and the modest Sharpe — all acceptable for a capital-preservation sleeve, not for a return-maximizing one.
